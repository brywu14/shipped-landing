  1. 01

    Add a tracking number.

    Paste or type a tracking number, choose the courier, and review it before saving. Shipments are stored on your iPhone.

    • Manual courier choice
    • Review before saving
    • Stored on-device
  2. 02

    One list. One journey each.

    Home shows what’s arriving and what’s on the way. Open a parcel for its status, estimate, route and a timeline of events, laid out so the latest news is first.

    • Shipment list
    • Status at a glance
    • Event timeline
  3. 03

    Find it later. Take it with you.

    Search by tracking number, courier or name, and look back through history. Export your saved shipments as a JSON file whenever you like. There’s no account to create.

    • Search
    • History
    • JSON export
    • No account
